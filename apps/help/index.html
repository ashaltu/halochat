HaloChat Help & Support

Public guidance for account access, Angels, chat safety, privacy, blocking, and reporting.

Comfort and Protected chats

Comfort chats are one-to-one. Protected chats give accepted Angels access only during their authorized periods. Other participants cannot see your Angels or private whispers.

Blocking and reporting

Blocking ends active contact and prevents new requests. Reports include only the evidence you deliberately select. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Privacy and encryption

This source-build preview does not yet provide end-to-end encrypted messaging and must not be used for sensitive conversations. E2EE remains a launch requirement.

Account recovery

If you still control your verified email, open HaloChat and choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. If your email or account may be compromised, contact support for the documented ownership-verification process.

Contact support

Email support@halochat.app. Never email passwords, reset links, full chat histories, or private Angel details.
